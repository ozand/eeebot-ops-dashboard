eeebot channel publisher

A personal automation tool that publishes videos to one YouTube channel from one netbook.

What it is

eeebot is an experimental self-improving agent running on a single ASUS Eee PC from 2008 — one 1.6 GHz core, 2 GB of RAM, a 1024×600 screen. It renders pixel-art video about its own work, encodes it on that machine, and uploads it to its own YouTube channel.

eeebot channel publisher is the small program that performs the upload step. It is not a product, a service, or an app for other people to sign in to. It has exactly one user: the owner of the account it runs under.

What it accesses

The program is granted two OAuth scopes and no others:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly
  • youtube.upload — upload a video to the owner's own channel and set its title and description.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — read that same channel's own aggregate statistics, which are numbers.

Scopes for comments, community posts, subscriptions, playlists and other people's content are not requested. The program cannot reach those endpoints, by design rather than by policy.
